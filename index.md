# Privacy Policy for Calcaloo Calorie Tracker

**Last updated:** January 10, 2026

## Overview

Calcaloo Calorie Tracker is designed to help you track your daily calorie intake, exercise, and view statistics. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Data Collection

We collect the following types of data:

**Personal Information:**
- Email address (required for account creation and authentication)
- - Name (optional, used as display name)
 
- **Health and Fitness Data:**
- - Calories burned (synced from Apple HealthKit or Google Health Connect with your permission)
   
- **Photos:**
- - Food photos (when you use the AI photo recognition feature)

- **Usage Data:**
- - App interactions and activity for analytics
- - Crash logs and diagnostics for app improvement
- - Device identifiers for push notifications and analytics

- ## How We Use Your Data

- - **Account Management:** Email and name are used to create and manage your account
- - **App Functionality:** Health data and photos are used to provide calorie tracking features
- - **AI Food Recognition:** Photos are sent to Anthropic API for AI analysis to identify food items
- - **Analytics:** Usage data helps us understand how the app is used and improve it
- - **Crash Reporting:** Diagnostic data helps us fix bugs and improve stability

- ## Third-Party Services

- We use the following third-party services:

- 1. **Firebase (Google):** Authentication, cloud database, analytics, and crash reporting
2. 2. **Anthropic API:** AI food recognition (photos are sent for analysis and are not stored permanently)
3. 3. **Apple HealthKit (iOS):** Read calories burned with your permission
4. 4. **Google Health Connect (Android):** Read calories burned with your permission

5. ## Data Storage and Security

6. - Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS
- - Account data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud)
- - Photos sent for AI analysis are processed and not stored permanently by Anthropic
- - Health data synced from HealthKit/Health Connect is used within the app

- ## Data Retention

- We retain your data as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

- ## Your Rights

- You have the right to:
- - Access your personal data
- - Request deletion of your data and account
- - Opt out of optional data collection (like health sync and AI features)

- ## Data Deletion

- To delete your account and all associated data, please contact us or use the account deletion feature in the app settings.

- ## Children's Privacy

- This app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

- ## Changes to This Policy

- We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
